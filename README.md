## 🔒 Our Core Privacy Commitment

- **Local-First Storage**: Your personal coordinates, sensor logs, and files remain on your device. We do not operate remote servers to harvest personal user tracking data.
- **Explicit Consent**: Sensitive device hardware (GPS, Microphone, Bluetooth) is only accessed with your permission for user-initiated tasks.
- **Compliance**: Fully aligned with Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and international data privacy regulations.
